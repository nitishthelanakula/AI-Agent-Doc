# Human Review Procedure

## Purpose

Some liquidity issues require judgement beyond automated threshold checks. Human review ensures that material judgement calls are validated by a knowledgeable reviewer before being reported.

## When human review is required

- Material variance from forecast or policy thresholds
- Limited confidence in source completeness or accuracy
- Potential covenant or legal entity limit breach
- Unexpected liquidity stress or funding strain
- Any manual override applied to a treasury report

## Procedure

1. Identify the impacted metric, entity, and reporting period.
2. Summarize the issue, source evidence, and business context.
3. Confirm whether the issue is operational, structural, or event-driven.
4. Record the review decision, owner, and next action.
5. Retain the supporting documentation for audit and sign-off.

## Final disposition

A human review outcome should be captured in the reporting record and reflected in the final narrative or briefing materials. The decision should be clear enough for a second reviewer to understand the rationale without further investigation.
