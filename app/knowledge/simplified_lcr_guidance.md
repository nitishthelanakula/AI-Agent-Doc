# Simplified LCR Guidance

## Formula

The liquidity coverage ratio is a simple measure of the relationship between available liquidity and expected cash outflows over a defined short-term horizon.

$$
\text{LCR} = \frac{\text{High-quality liquid assets} + \text{cash inflows}}{\text{cash outflows}}
$$

## Interpretation

- A ratio above 1.0 indicates that available liquidity exceeds expected outflows.
- A ratio below 1.0 suggests that short-term liquidity may be constrained.
- A ratio materially below policy thresholds requires escalation and review.

## Practical application

Treasury teams should use the most recent validated cash and flow data when calculating the ratio. In operational practice, the result should be supplemented with qualitative review of funding concentration, source reliability, and event-driven cash movements.

## Notes

This guidance is intended as simplified operational guidance. Final regulatory or policy interpretation should follow the formal treasury policy documentation and relevant governing standards.
