# Anomaly Review Guidance

## Purpose

Anomaly review is used to identify movements or values that are unusually large, inconsistent, or unexplained relative to the normal operating pattern.

## Typical triggers

- A large balance movement without corresponding transaction detail
- A sudden shift in expected inflows or outflows
- A material variance from prior-period or forecast values
- A source system value that is inconsistent with other treasury data sources

## Review steps

1. Validate whether the anomaly is supported by transaction history or known operational events.
2. Confirm whether the value is sourced from an approved system or requires a manual adjustment.
3. Check whether policy thresholds, risk limits, or covenant metrics are impacted.
4. Document the conclusion and any required correction or escalation.

## Escalation

Where the anomaly affects the liquidity position, covenant metrics, or stakeholder reporting, it should be escalated to the treasury lead for human review before the report is finalized.
