FAQ 1

How do I request a standard contract review?

  1. Open the Contract Lifecycle Portal and select New Legal Review.
  2. Choose the contract type and business jurisdiction.
  3. Upload the editable draft, counterparty paper, commercial summary and approval record.
  4. Select Standard priority and submit. The portal-generated request ID is the citation and tracking reference.
FAQ 2

How do I request an urgent contract review?

  1. Create the request in the Contract Lifecycle Portal using the same mandatory documents.
  2. Select Expedited priority and provide the required signature date, business impact, contract value band and reason standard timing is insufficient.
  3. Add the tag URGENT-LEGAL and obtain approval from the accountable business director.
  4. Email the portal request ID to the Legal Operations contact below. Do not send the contract as an email attachment if it is already in the portal.
FAQ 3

What are the turnaround times?

  • Standard review: initial legal response within five business days after a complete request is received.
  • Expedited review: initial legal response within one business day after priority approval and receipt of a complete request.
  • Turnaround measures the initial review response, not final contract signature. Complex negotiations, missing documents or specialist review may extend completion time.

FAQ 4

When should I use expedited review?

Use expedited review only for a documented regulatory deadline, an externally committed signature date, an active service-risk event, or a material customer escalation. Lack of internal planning alone is not an urgency reason.
